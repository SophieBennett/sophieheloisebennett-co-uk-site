About Me

I’m Sophie, a data leader with ten years’ experience spanning data science, analytics and data engineering. My particular focus is product data science: helping teams understand how people use products, make better decisions about what to build and measure whether those decisions worked.

My career has taken me through education technology, sports technology and, most recently, online retail at Butternut Box. I’ve worked across the data lifecycle, from building reliable data foundations and analytics capabilities to applying experimentation and modelling to product questions. I’m most interested in the point where technical work meets real decisions—connecting data, people and products in a way that is useful rather than merely impressive.

I originally studied experimental psychology and have a PhD in neuroscience. That background still shapes how I approach data: with curiosity about human behaviour, care around measurement and a healthy scepticism about easy answers.

This site is home to practical writing about data, experimentation and analytics, alongside a Playground for smaller, more light-hearted investigations and visualisations.
